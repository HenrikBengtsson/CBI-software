Cell Ranger is proprietary software from 10x Genomics, and it is not
installed on this cluster. This module exists only to explain why, so
that you do not have to guess whether we forgot about it.

It is not clear to us whether the Cell Ranger end-user license
agreement (EULA) allows us to install the software once and make it
available to everyone here. It raises at a few questions that we are
uncertain about. For example, the license is granted to one named
licensee, and it is non-transferable and non-sublicensable (Section
3.1). Also, access may not be given to anyone other than the
employees, agents, and contractors of that licensee, and each of them
has to be bound by the terms of the EULA separately (Section
4.1(b)).

Until that is clear, we take the conservative approach and do not
install Cell Ranger for everyone.

What you can do instead is to install Cell Ranger under your own
account, where you accept the EULA yourself:

  <https://www.10xgenomics.com/support/software/cell-ranger/downloads>

If your lab or your department holds a license that covers a shared
installation on this cluster, or know someone who can investigate this
problem further, please get in touch.
