This software is not installed on this cluster, and this module exists
only to explain why.

It is not clear to us whether the end-user license agreement (EULA) of
the software allows us to install it once and make it available to
everyone here. Until that is clear, we take the conservative approach
and do not install it for everyone. What you can do instead is to
install it under your own account, where you accept the EULA yourself.

If your lab or your department holds a license that covers a shared
installation on this cluster, please get in touch and we will revisit
this:
